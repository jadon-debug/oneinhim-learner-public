App prep checklist

Mux steps

  • Upload the full approved video to Mux.
  • Wait until the asset is ready.
  • Paste the playback ID here.
  • Use timestamp ranges to place moments into the learner journey.

Rules for video

  • Do not manually cut the source video for journey clips.
  • One source video can create many virtual clips.
  • Early section videos should be short and clear.
  • Longer teaching belongs further along the journey.
  • Every clip should answer a real question or lead to one next step.
  • Discover can show the full video; Journey should use the best learning moment.

Team test sync

Free-tier setup: Mux stores the video, Supabase stores the journey records, and the hosted learner app reads them automatically.
